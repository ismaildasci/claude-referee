ROADMAP (Ideas, negative result): Code-only Stop note measured and not adopted. On 193 usable study sessions the strict keyword reading caught 11 of 16 wrong dones (Jev gate 15 of 16) because 4 wrong dones hedge in the same message; the lenient reading catches 15 of 16 but flags every correct claim without a counted check (152 of 152 asked, precision 0.09). A lenient "edits without a counted pass" reminder as an opt-in mode needs its own registration and a decision on whether it is worth shipping; see docs/decisions/stop-code-note.md.
